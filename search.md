## How to search

1. **Query the stem, not the full title.** `Produktdesign` catches Produktdesigner,
   Produktdesignerin and Produktdesigner*in. `Computational Design` catches Computational
   Designer and Specialist Computational Design.
2. **Filter the results by hand** against the titles and exclusions below.
3. Run full spellings only on boards that match exactly, or when a stem returns nothing.
4. **Search tool names on their own** (e.g. `Rhino`, `Figma`, `Python`). They surface ads
   whose title says nothing useful.
5. On non-English boards, **search English titles too**. Many postings use them verbatim.

## Excluded

<!-- Drop rules. Be specific about wording, so the routine doesn't have to guess. -->

- **Multi-year experience demands.** Drop postings that ask for <3+> years
  (e.g. "3+ years", "proven track record", *mehrjährige / langjährige Berufserfahrung*).
  **Keep** anything asking for ≤ 2 years, "first experience", soft wording ("ideally"), or
  nothing at all.
- **Job types:** <e.g. internships, working-student, apprenticeships>
- **Role types:** <e.g. roles where tool X is the whole job; pure drafting roles>
