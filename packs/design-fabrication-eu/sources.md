## Leads, not boards

Most good roles in this field are never advertised. These rarely post, or post only on
their own sites; approach them directly with a portfolio and a specific idea.

- **Digital-fabrication studios:** Aectual, Studio RAP, The New Raw, Vertico (NL) ·
  Design-to-Production, ROK (CH) · Odico, GXN (DK) · Noumena, Nagami (ES)
- **3D printing / robotic fabrication:** trinckle, BigRep, Xolo (Berlin) · Ai Build,
  Hyperion Robotics, WASP · Formlabs, UltiMaker
- **Mass customisation:** orthopaedics (Ottobock, Össur, Invent Medical, Spentys) ·
  footwear/eyewear (Zellerfeld, YOU MAWO, HEXR) · configurators (Tylko, Roomle, Threekit,
  ShapeDiver, Vectary)
- **Exhibition and stage fabrication (Berlin):** Museumstechnik, Duncan McCauley,
  Bühnenservice Berlin; ATELIER BRÜCKNER (Stuttgart)
- **Artist and media-art studios (Berlin):** Studio Olafur Eliasson, Studio Tomás Saraceno,
  ART+COM, WHITEvoid, onformative, Random International
- **Rhino-heavy industries with other job titles:** aircraft and yacht interiors (Lufthansa
  Technik, Lürssen, Feadship), timber prefab (Blumer-Lehmann, WIEHAG, Züblin Timber),
  automotive design studios, sports equipment
- **Research institutes that hire Master's graduates:** Fraunhofer IPK / IAPT / IPA,
  Matters of Activity, Weizenbaum-Institut
- **Funding instead of a salary:** EXIST-Gründerstipendium, Prototype Fund, S+T+ARTS
  residencies
- **Events where hiring happens:** formnext (Frankfurt, November), ACADIA, Fabricate,
  Advances in Architectural Geometry, CDFAM
