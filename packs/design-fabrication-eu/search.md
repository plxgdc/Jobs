## How to search

1. **Query the stem, not the full title.** `Produktdesign` catches Produktdesigner,
   Produktdesignerin, Produktdesigner*in. `Computational Design` catches Computational
   Designer and Specialist Computational Design.
2. **Filter the results by hand** against the titles and exclusions below.
3. Run full spellings only on exact-match boards (Arbeitsagentur), or when a stem returns nothing.
4. **Search English titles on German boards too** (Creative Technologist, Design Engineer).
5. **Search tool names on their own** (`Rhino`, `Grasshopper`): they surface ads whose title
   says nothing useful, e.g. timber-construction planning roles.
6. **Search facility words**: universities and museums title technical posts by the
   workshop (`Metallwerkstatt`, `Holzwerkstatt`, `Modellwerkstatt`), not by the skill.

## Excluded

- **Multi-year experience demands**: *mehrjährige / langjährige / fundierte
  Berufserfahrung*, *mind. 3 Jahre*, "3+ years", "proven track record",
  *eigenverantwortliche Projektleitung*. **Keep** ≤ 2 years, *erste Berufserfahrung*,
  soft wording, or nothing stated.
- **Senior / Lead / Principal / Head of / Leitung** titles, unless the ad names no
  experience requirement.
- Internships, Werkstudent, Ausbildung, Volontariat.
- **BIM as the core role** (BIM-Koordination, BIM-Management). A design role that lists
  Revit among its tools is reported with the gap flagged.
- Interior architecture as the core role; generic facade drafting; pure mechanical CAD.
